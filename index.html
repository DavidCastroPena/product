01 · Overview
How to talk to users
IGCPharma.com

A framework inspired by Gustaf Alströmer (YC Partner) on conducting user interviews that generate real insight — not false validation.

1
Find the right users — they'll keep you honestThe people who tell you hard truths are more valuable than those who tell you what you want to hear.
2
Face users directly — don't hide behind messagesThe key to user growth is showing up. No-reply emails kill learning.
3
Listen first, build secondYour job in these conversations is to understand problems, not pitch solutions.
02 · Getting in the room
Writing outreach that works
IGCPharma.com

Gustaf's messages work because they lead with genuine connection, not a pitch.

Gustaf → CFO at Motion Inc
"Hi! This is Gustaf from Airbnb. We overlapped in 2014 when you were on the Finance team… I'm talking to people like yourself to learn as much as possible about how companies think about carbon emissions. Would you have time for a 20 min call?"
What works
  • Shared history / connection
  • Clear, bounded ask (20 min)
  • Framed as learning, not selling
  • Specific topic area
What doesn't
  • Introducing your product
  • Open-ended "pick your time"
  • Generic "I'd love to connect"
  • Sending from a no-reply
03 · The golden rules
Don't introduce your product
IGCPharma.com
"Your job is to understand their world, not explain yours."
👂
Listen, don't talkAim for 20% speaking, 80% listening. Silence after a response often prompts the most honest answers.
🔓
Ask open-ended questions onlyYes/no questions shut conversations down. Open questions reveal mental models you didn't know existed.
📺
Ask them to screenshareWatching someone actually try to solve the problem reveals 10× more than what they say about it.
04 · The interview script
Questions that unlock insight
IGCPharma.com
01Tell me how you do X today.
02What is the hardest thing about doing X?
03Why is it hard?
04How often do you have to do X?
05Why is it important for your company to do X?
Follow up every answer with: "What do you mean by that?" · "Can you tell me more?" · "Why is this important to you?"
05 · Traps to avoid
Questions that waste the call
IGCPharma.com

Users have good problems but often bad solutions. They have no incentive to say no to a feature — so stop asking about features.

✗Will you use our product?
✗Which features would make it better?
✗Yes / no questions
✗How would a better product look?
✗Two questions at the same time
✗Hypotheticals about the future
"Users have great problems. They often have terrible solutions. Focus on the problem."
06 · After the conversation
Synthesize, then build
IGCPharma.com
🗒️

Synthesize learnings

Document verbatim quotes and patterns across interviews

💡

Problem–solution hypothesis

Write one crisp sentence: "People with X problem need Y"

✏️

Sketch the MVP

Only the smallest thing that tests your hypothesis

Before building, validate the problem is worth solving:

GreenAre people paying money for solutions in this space today?
GreenHave users tried to solve this on their own?
SignalSome customers will always be easier to sell to — who?
07 · After the MVP
Observe, don't tell them what to do
IGCPharma.com
👀

Watch them solve the problem

Don't explain the product. Let them use it. Where they get stuck is your roadmap.

💬

Prompt them to speak their mind

"Tell me what you're thinking right now." Silence is not allowed — narration reveals assumptions.

🔒

Create an exclusive channel

Make early users feel special. A private Slack, early access, a direct line to you — they give more when they feel invested.

🤝

Connect them with each other

Users in the same problem space love meeting each other. You become the hub — and learn even more.

12 · Consumer segment
Who they are — 4 profiles
IGCPharma.com

Recruit for variation in age, health engagement, and proximity to a diagnosis. Do not screen for brain health awareness — most people don't have a framework for it yet, and that's exactly what you're here to learn.

Worried well (45–65)

n = 3

Family history, no symptoms yet. High health literacy. Proactive buyers of preventive products. Will pay for peace of mind.

Caregiver / family member

n = 3

Actively managing a loved one's cognitive decline. Emotionally invested, high unmet need. Strong word-of-mouth reach.

Health optimizer (30–50)

n = 2

Uses wearables, tracks sleep, experiments with nootropics. Already spending on imperfect solutions — reveals the WTP ceiling.

Post-diagnosis patient

n = 2

MCI or early Alzheimer's diagnosis. Seeking tools to slow progression. Tests clinical utility and surfaces accessibility needs.

Where to find them: Caregiver Facebook groups, r/Alzheimers, r/longevity, memory cafés, Nextdoor, one-degree referrals from your own network.
13 · Consumer screener
Qualifying before the call
IGCPharma.com

Screen for health-engaged people — not brain-health-aware people. That distinction doesn't exist yet for most users. You need at least 2 "yes" answers to proceed.

Q1Do you actively track any aspect of your health? (e.g. steps, sleep, weight, blood pressure, diet)
Q2Have you ever felt like you weren't getting enough information about what's actually happening inside your body?
Q3Do you see a doctor or specialist for preventive care — not just when something is wrong?
Q4Have you or someone close to you dealt with a health condition that was hard to monitor or caught later than you'd like?
Consumer outreach template
Hi [Name] — I'm a researcher studying how people stay on top of their health and what makes it hard. I'm not pitching anything — just trying to understand the experience. Would you have 25 minutes to talk?
14 · Consumer interview guide
Questions for consumers
IGCPharma.com
Phase 1 — How they track health today
01Walk me through how you keep track of your health. What do you monitor, if anything?context
02How did you end up doing it that way? What made you start — or not start?behavior
Phase 2 — Frictions & gaps
03What's the hardest thing about knowing if you're actually healthy — not just not sick, but genuinely doing well?pain
04Tell me about a time you felt something was off. How did you figure out what it was? What did you do?behavior
05What do you wish you could check or track that you currently can't — or that feels too difficult or expensive?gap
Phase 3 — Priority scan
06Imagine you could do a full health scan once a year — anything at all, not just blood work. What would you most want to know?priority scan
07You mentioned [X] — why that over everything else? What would knowing that change for you?why
08Are you paying for anything today to stay on top of your health — devices, apps, specialists, supplements?willingness to pay
If brain / cognition hasn't come up by Q6: "What about how your brain or memory is doing — is that something you think about?" Note whether it surfaces naturally or only when introduced.

15 · Provider segment
Who they are — 5 profiles
IGCPharma.com

Providers reveal what people already pay for, what gaps exist in delivery, and which institutions could become distribution partners or customers.

Memory clinic / neurologist

n = 3

Clinical authority. Unmet patient education and between-visit monitoring needs. Potential referral engine.

Wellness studio / brain gym

n = 2

BrainHQ-style operators, mindfulness + cognitive studios. Consumer-facing. Shows what the market accepts today.

Senior living / adult day center

n = 2

Operations staff managing residents' cognitive engagement. Institutional buyer with group licensing potential.

Primary care / integrative medicine

n = 2

First touchpoint before diagnosis. Drives referrals. Interested in preventive tools that extend the visit.

Corporate wellness / HR

n = 1

Benefits buyers with budget. Move fast when confirmed. Reaching 1 buyer can unlock hundreds of users.

After 20 interviews — what to build
IGCPharma.com
🗒️

Capture verbatim

Write down exact words — not summaries. The user's language is your copy.

🔁

Find 3+ repeats

Any pain or behavior mentioned by 3+ people across both segments is signal.

💡

Write the hypothesis

"People with [X] need [Y] but today they [workaround]."

Green lights before building:

GreenPeople are already paying for imperfect brain health solutions
GreenUsers or providers built their own workarounds
GreenThe problem was mentioned before you asked about it
SignalThey introduced you to someone else with the same problem
